# Enerkoro Color Customization

Battle GUI now lets the player choose a visual background color independently for Enerkoro 1, 2, and 3.

- Choices are restricted to Energy element colors used by the game.
- The selected colors are cosmetic only and never change Energy faces, rolls, or battle logic.
- Each choice is persisted in `user://ui_preferences.cfg`.
- The selected background is visible in DICE READY, rolling, and landed states.
- Charakoro remains neutral.
- The previous automatic “color by rolled Energy result” toggle has been removed.
