# Free Mode - Repeated Fixed Energy

Free Mode now includes an optional **Allow repeated Fixed Energy** toggle.

Behavior:
- The toggle is visible only in Free Mode.
- OFF (default): existing rule remains unchanged; Fixed Energy types must be unique across all three Enerkoro.
- ON: the same Fixed Energy type may be used on different Enerkoro.
- The two Fixed faces on the same Enerkoro must still use different Energy types.
- The setting is carried from Battle Preparation into the Enerkoro Builder and into battle runtime validation.
- Leaving Free Mode resets the option to OFF. Starting a new Free Mode session also defaults it to OFF.

The option is shown in both Battle Preparation's Enerkoro panel and the Enerkoro Builder for convenience.
